# Knowledge Promotion

Use within an authorized maintenance, Batch acceptance, or Epoch operation,
or when the user explicitly asks to promote measured conclusions. Respect
operation-specific limits such as keeping the result local.

Separate measured facts, interpretations, decisions, and open questions.
Promote each useful claim to exactly one source, test, contract, architecture,
plan, decision, constraint, glossary, or validated experience owner. Change that
owner materially; a link to equivalent existing text adds no new knowledge.
Discard routine commands, duplicate prose, task history, and local observations
that do not establish a reusable engineering fact. Keep unresolved decisions
open until their closure evidence exists.

A change to product meaning or ownership needs its canonical decision; a
repository-wide replacement belongs to an explicitly requested Epoch. Ordinary
extraction does not trigger governance. Report a brief claim/owner/evidence
mapping in conversation, with no separate record or grading vocabulary.
